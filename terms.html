Effective Date: September 1, 2026 | Republic of Kenya

1. General Membership & Association Framework

By accessing Calcare platforms, registering your mobile number, or applying for financial loan facilities, you agree to become a recognized member of the Calcare Welfare & Financial Association. Membership grants you access to prioritized emergency assistance, community welfare funds, and micro-loan services.

2. Monthly Welfare Contribution (KSh 100)

To maintain active membership standing and ensure continuous eligibility for community benefits and loan approvals, an association welfare fee of KSh 100 is contributed monthly. This contribution is handled separately from loan disbursements and principal repayments.

🛡️ 100% Refundability Policy: Should a member decide to step out or discontinue association membership, all paid welfare contributions are 100% refundable upon submission of a valid request to our support desk.
3. Loan Facilities & Disbursals

Loan offers provided on Calcare are tailored based on ID verification, member tier, and financial assessment. Approved loan disbursements are processed directly to the member's registered M-Pesa mobile number following successful account activation and administrative clearance.

4. Repayment Schedule & Obligations

Members agree to settle their repayment installments in accordance with the schedule displayed upon loan selection. Early settlements carry zero penalty and automatically elevate the member's credit limit for subsequent borrowing cycles.

5. Member Conduct & Dispute Resolution

All disputes, refund inquiries, or membership status modifications are resolved transparently through our customer support desk under Kenyan financial and consumer protection laws.
