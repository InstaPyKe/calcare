Privacy Policy

Compliant with Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019

1. Data Protection Commitment

At Calcare, your privacy and personal data integrity are paramount. We strictly process and protect all personal and financial data under the provisions of the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 and international data security standards.

2. Information We Collect

To evaluate loan limits and deliver welfare services, we collect:
• Full legal name and verified National ID numbers.
• Contact details (phone number, email address, place of birth).
• M-Pesa transaction verification receipts and payment confirmations.
• Device parameters strictly utilized for anti-fraud security authentication.

3. Bank-Grade Encryption & Confidentiality

All transmission channels utilize 256-bit SSL encryption. We enforce a zero-tolerance policy against the unauthorized sale, lease, or unauthorized sharing of member information with commercial marketing entities.

4. Your Rights as a Data Subject

You hold the legal right to request access to your collected personal records, demand correction of discrepancies, or request account data removal upon complete settlement of loan facilities and association exit.
